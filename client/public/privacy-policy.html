2. Information We Collect

When you browse, register, or order from GharTak Kart, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, 10-digit mobile phone number, and optional email address.
  • Delivery Address & Precise Location: Your house/flat number, street name, landmarks, and GPS latitude/longitude coordinates (with your permission) to calculate distance from our Khair store and dispatch delivery personnel accurately.
  • Order & Cart Details: Selected grocery and food items, order value, delivery instructions, and order history.
  • Payment Details: We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and direct UPI QR payments. We never collect, store, or process your credit/debit card numbers or UPI MPINs.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version (Android/Browser), IP address, and basic crash logs to maintain app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. To process, pack, and deliver your groceries and food items within 15–25 minutes.
  2. To calculate delivery distance from our Somna Road, Khair store hub and apply appropriate fee slabs.
  3. To send real-time order tracking updates and receipts via WhatsApp and SMS.
  4. To track your Loyalty Stamp Card progress (1 stamp on ₹250+ order; FREE Surprise Gift on 10 stamps).
  5. To provide prompt doorstep customer support, refunds, or grievance resolution.
